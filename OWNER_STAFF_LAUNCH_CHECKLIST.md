# Kridiya Owner and Staff Launch Checklist

Use this as the operating method before launch, every working day, and during monthly owner review.

## Final Launch Check

1. Open Dashboard and confirm system health is acceptable.
2. Open Enquiries and confirm every new or awaiting-quote enquiry has an owner action.
3. Open Customers and confirm customer profiles are linked correctly by email.
4. Open Bookings and confirm no confirmed booking is missing payment control.
5. Open Payments and confirm customer payments, supplier costs, and refunds are clear.
6. Open Documents and confirm invoices, tickets, vouchers, visa notices, and templates are ready.
7. Open Staff and confirm only required staff are active.
8. Open Activity and review recent login, payment, refund, staff, document, and backup events.
9. Open Backups and download the full backup pack.
10. Save exports into the SharePoint/archive structure before treating the system as launch-ready.

## Daily Staff Workflow

1. Start from Dashboard.
2. Clear priority work in this order:
   - unpaid confirmed bookings
   - pending refunds
   - overdue booking tasks
   - documents pending
   - open enquiries
3. For each enquiry:
   - confirm customer contact details
   - confirm service, route/destination, travel dates, passengers, and notes
   - send quote or request missing details
4. For each accepted quote:
   - convert to booking
   - link customer
   - record payment requirement
   - add supplier and document tasks
5. For each booking:
   - collect or verify payment before supplier confirmation when required
   - record supplier payable and supplier reference
   - upload internal files
   - release only customer-ready files
6. Before closing the day:
   - complete due tasks
   - record handover notes
   - check Dashboard warnings again

## Owner Weekly Review

1. Review finance summary.
2. Review pending customer payments.
3. Review supplier payables and disputed supplier items.
4. Approve or reject refund requests.
5. Review corporate accounts, LPO status, and billing risk.
6. Review staff permissions and recent activity.
7. Check whether any file or booking needs archive cleanup.

## Owner Monthly Review

1. Open Backups.
2. Download all CSV exports.
3. Save backup pack into:
   `Kridiya Travel/05 Staff and Security/Monthly Backups/YYYY-MM`
4. Save finance exports into:
   `Kridiya Travel/02 Finance/YYYY/MM/Accounting Export`
5. Review Activity for unusual payment, refund, staff, security, document, and export events.
6. Review staff list and remove or reduce permissions for inactive users.
7. Confirm business settings: legal name, trade licence, bank details, VAT/TRN status, cancellation/refund wording.

## Emergency Fallback

If the admin system is unavailable:

1. Use the latest backup pack as the operating record.
2. Work from exported bookings, customers, payments, documents, and activity files.
3. Use Outlook/WhatsApp templates from the Templates page if already copied, or the latest SOP wording.
4. Record all manual changes in a temporary spreadsheet.
5. When the system returns, enter the manual changes into admin and upload supporting documents.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Do not share internal folders directly with customers.
- Do not release a document until it is final and customer-ready.
- Do not mark a booking complete if payment, supplier, document, or refund status is unclear.
- Do not give staff broad permissions unless their role requires it.
- Do not delete business records unless the owner has approved it.
