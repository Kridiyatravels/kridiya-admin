Controlled response

1. Find affected bookings

Preview only. Nothing is created or communicated until you select bookings and open the disruption.

2. Open controlled response

Selected bookings become the fixed affected set; an operational exception and urgent task are created automatically.

3. Review and publish updates

Drafts stay internal. Publishing adds a customer-safe portal update and records the exact audience and message.
