# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Kridiya Travel staff and owners in the UAE use this private operations tool throughout the working day. They review customer enquiries, prepare and compare travel quotes, convert accepted work into bookings, coordinate documents and suppliers, record payments, and monitor operational follow-up.

## Product Purpose

Kridiya Staff Tools is the internal operating system for Kridiya Travel and Tourism FZ-LLC. It keeps the enquiry-to-booking workflow structured and auditable while helping staff respond quickly through WhatsApp and email. Success means a staff member can understand the next action, prepare an accurate customer-ready quote, and move the enquiry forward without duplicate entry or ambiguity.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, precise. The interface should feel like a dependable travel operations console: familiar, efficient, and clearly connected to the Kridiya Travel brand.

## Anti-references

- Generic marketing or SaaS landing-page layouts inside operational screens.
- Decorative cards, gradients, or animation that slow down repeated staff work.
- Fake live-booking behavior or unclear states that imply a quote is saved before it is.
- Dense controls without clear labels, validation, or a visible next action.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next operational action obvious.
2. Keep customer-facing information precise and ready to share.
3. Use familiar controls and consistent states across every module.
4. Prevent accidental sends, duplicate records, and destructive mistakes.
5. Preserve context while staff move from enquiry to quote to booking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard operation, visible focus states, clear text validation, and layouts usable at laptop and mobile widths. Respect reduced-motion preferences and never rely on color alone to communicate status.
